The Document Ingestion Resilience backend uses `pypdfium2` for bounded PDF page rendering. The pypdfium2 bindings are distributed under BSD-3-Clause or Apache-2.0 terms and load a PDFium binary with additional permissive dependency notices. The PyInstaller specification copies the complete license directory shipped by the installed pypdfium2 wheel to `third-party-licenses/pypdfium2` in every frozen backend bundle. Those packaged files must not be removed from redistributed builds.

LibreOffice and Tesseract are not bundled. When an owner opts into legacy DOC conversion or OCR, Job Apply Pro invokes the separately installed executable at the exact configured path. Owners and redistributors remain responsible for installing and using those tools under their respective licenses.
